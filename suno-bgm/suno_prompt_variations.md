# Suno Prompt Variations

作成日: 2026-10-01

## Recommended VLOG Piano Prompt

Style:

```text
Gentle cinematic piano instrumental for calm VLOG and short videos.

A beautiful, simple and memorable piano theme.
Quiet and minimal, but with a strong melodic identity.

Warm, nostalgic, contemplative and slightly bittersweet.
Slow tempo around 70 BPM.
Delicate solo piano with subtle sustained background textures.

The first few seconds should immediately introduce the main melody.
The melody should work well in YouTube Shorts and VLOG clips.

Minimal arrangement with plenty of space between notes.
Soft dynamics, delicate touch, intimate atmosphere.

Suitable for daily VLOGs, travel, walking videos,
quiet local scenery, historical places, shrines and temples,
flowers, nature, calm city scenes, morning light,
peaceful scenery and reflective YouTube Shorts.
```

Duration:

```text
Around 3 minutes.
The track should work as background music for videos between 1:00 and 2:50.
It should remain calm and usable throughout, with no sudden restart feeling.
```

Exclude:

```text
vocals, lyrics, drums, percussion, aggressive bass,
epic orchestra, dramatic climax, cinematic trailer,
fast arpeggios, jazz, upbeat rhythm, sudden changes,
strong genre identity, traditional Japanese instruments,
religious ritual music, tourist commercial music
```

Title examples:

```text
Quiet Reverie 01
Soft Afterglow 01
Gentle Resonance 01
Still Morning 01
Slow Day Piano 01
```
